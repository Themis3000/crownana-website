8/1/26 What It Means!

A fellow website friend I met online glumpups just released a new ep with their band What It Means! I've been really excited for its release ever since it was announced. It's really excellent, you should give it a listen! It's available on bandcamp and on spotify . They all worked really hard on it and it shows. I've been listening to it a lot ever since it released a few days ago!

And of course since I consume most of my music through cds now, I printed and burned a cd for it!

I hope one day they make an official one I can replace this one with :)

My favorite track is "The Long Way". (I assume) it's about deciding to intentionally take your joy in life into your own hands, and finding what makes life exciting. The lyric "Nothing ventured nothing learned. It's not the wrong way, it's just the long way. Please don't turn around" has really stuck around in my head. I like how it reframes perceived failure as just "the long way", not a wrong turn or a mistake. And that giving up would be like turning back for home despite you being well on your way just because a trip was longer then you expected.

I hope you go check it out :). They're located in the UK, if you happen to be reading from there you might be able to catch one of their shows. They've been able to perform live a few times! I wish I could go see one of their performances live.


As an update to the last post, that big file transfer finally finished. I was originally migrating to Mega's S4 storage on the account of it only being $10/month for 3tb. However, as files uploaded my upload speed started to get throttled. Eventually it was so slow that it was going to take over 200 days to complete!! If you too find yourself with over 1 million files to store, I really cannot recommend Mega. That was obviously unworkable, so I switched over to Wasabi. They're a bit more expensive at $8/tb, but at least I was actually able to move all my files over to them. It ended up taking about 3 days to complete the transfer to Wasabi.
