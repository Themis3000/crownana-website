And for her sister I made this set of pins:


But these aren't just pins, they are working nfc tags! The top left directs to her instagram when scanned, the right goes to this website, and the bottom one is a working amiibo. There's a trick to making these, when pressing the pin together if you just put the nfc tag sticker directly on the metal backing it won't work at all. The metal backing interferes with the radio signal that comes off of the tag. You need a material that reflects the radio signal so that it doesn't reach the metal backing. I got a sheet of adhesive ferrite which I evenly apply to the metal backing, then I apply the nfc sticker on top of that, then I press the rest of the pin as normal. After doing that it'll work as a normal nfc tag! To my knowledge no one else has done this before with a pin specifically. With how many people are in the world it probably has been done, but I like to think the few I have and the 3 I gave to her are the only ones in existence of their kind.
