A new start!

Hello, world!

It looks like this is a new start on the internet for me. Over the last few years, I've wanted to "put stuff out there" in a variety of different ways. Or at least, I've wanted to have my own little void to talk my ideas into. I wasn't sure how to fill such a desire yet, until a couple of youtube videos came around and inspired me.

A video called "You Should Check Out the Inde Web" took a look into the scene of people maintaining personal websites during the modern day. They spoke about the limitations of social media, web3 "disruptors" and their ever present drive to monetize everything and taylor everything to you. By comparison, this small scene of personal websites is a chance to look into other's little corners they've built up complete with their own unique little fixations, diaries, and fun little tidbits. The content you can find is generally made by the creator for the creator, not made for a specific person, purpose, or for monetization. They describe the means of which to browse the "Inde Web", how discovery happens, and how you can go about building your own site and join the community.

I'm not sure that I'll be joining any "Inde Web" groups yet, but it was a fascinating insight into a community that really made me consider the idea of creating my own website. You can watch the video here if you're curious to see it yourself

The second video I saw that gave me the final shove to start work on this site was a video called "Why YOU Should Make a Website!". In this video they spoke about the hostility and the ongoing enshittification of social media, propelled by the age of ai and intense advertising/monetization. They then present their proposed solution to the problem, which is to just make your own space where you don't need to worry about any algorithm or trends to follow. They describe how compared to a social media profile, a personal website is incredibly potent with personality and information.

This second video is what made me want to go start work on my website right away! I realized what I've been missing in my internet experience is my own little corner where I can avoid the "line go up" and monetization culture of social media. I can put whatever I want up, customize the interface to reflect my own personality, and run free without limits. Their video is available here if you are interested.

Over time, I'll probably be adding things here and there. It's not going to be impressive or professional, but it'll be my little corner. I'll just write whatever I feel like. Tutorials, opinions, how my day was, and whatever else. Some of it will maybe be interesting and some of it maybe dull. As of now, I consider the site to be in a state that isn't busy enough for my liking. I'm definitely going to find ways to busy up the interface with fun stuff over time.

Well, thanks for coming and enjoy your stay! Remember to stay weird :)
