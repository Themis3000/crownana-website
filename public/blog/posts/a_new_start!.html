A new start!

Hello, world!

It looks like this is a new start on the internet for me. Over the last few years, I've wanted to "put stuff out there" in a variety of different ways. Or at least, I've wanted to have my own little void to talk into. I wasn't sure how to fill such a desire yet, until a couple of youtube videos came around and inspired me.

I didn’t want a space where I would be limited to a particular format by a platform, and I didn’t want to be forced into letting old things die at the bottom of a list of posts. I wanted to be able to bake my personal style into every part of my presence, and have the ability to keep things I’m proud of prominently displayed.

You Should Check Out the Inde Web and Why YOU Should Make a Website! reminded me that the best way to keep total control over your online presence is to simply maintain your own website. When I was young I remember youtube channels having full page themes that where sick as hell. Now social media sites are just uninspiring white and gray with rounded corners on every element displayed. I mean, come on. Bring this back You cannot tell me this isn’t sick as hell!

You just can't do anything like this on social media anymore. Even most website building tools these days confine you into a modern style and don’t give you the ability to really run wild.

I’ve specifically designed this website to be a giant middle finger in the face of modern web design tendencies, as well as a homage to all the silly, alternative, and downright crazy personal web pages of years past.

Thank you for coming and enjoy your stay! Remember to stay weird :)
