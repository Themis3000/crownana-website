This ostrich was my favorite animal at the zoo when I visited. He has such a personality. He's in a pen with three giraffes, it's like the "long neck" enclosure. He walks around with his beak open 90% of the time. He'll also lay down and take dust baths, throwing up huge amounts of dust in the air and rubbing his face on the ground.


I watched the ostrich for a long time. Eventually, he approached the fence to grab some food and get a better look at me and my partner. He'd eat large bites out of his food dish in the same way a duck eats with a lot of quick, rather harmless looking nips. Because of his size, he'd eat an impressive amount of food per bite. He went between eating his food and nipping at the fence between us. I'm not sure why he was doing this, he didn't hiss or make any sounds. Sometimes he'd do a trot around, then return to nip and eat again.


Even the giraffes where confused by this behavior. As he did this, all three giraffes were watching him. I could see paint rubbed off in the areas on the fence he was nipping, so I think this may be a common behavior for him.


To me, it seems this bird has the personality of a harmless rascal. I quite enjoy seeing what he's up to.
