The light rail arriving

I took this photo with a long shutter speed (1/10th of a second) purposely in order to capture the motion blur. Although some parts of the photo came out unideal compared to what I wanted, the reflections on the side of the train came out very interesting in my opinion.

The whole side of the train is reflective, but a lot of the panels are angled slightly differently or are warped. Pay attention to the reflection of the walk-sign number, you can see the "8" in 3 different places. I think it's pretty cool seeing a visual representation of how the reflections dance in little ways on the side of the train as it zooms by within the span of 1/10 of a second.
